# Status after the reviewer's next-phase program (2026-11-21)

This file is the single entry point for the state of the replacement study after the
second review round (`m02980`). Everything below is reproducible from a clean checkout:
`python scripts/replay_replacement.py` (CPU) rebuilds every committed number at 1e-6 and
`python scripts/check_claims.py` re-checks each quoted value against its artefact.

## What the frozen system is

Dense `mxbai-embed-large-v1` top-100 + SFT cross-encoder (`v2m3_pool8_e2`, 2 epochs,
lr 1.5e-5), z-normalised 1:1 fusion, deterministic tie-break. Dev domain-macro
**0.3580985233** (13 domains, 519 queries), independently re-verified on 2026-11-20.
Checkpoint, truncation, weights and hashes are pinned in
`results/FROZEN_BASELINE_20261120.json` and `configs/replacement_study_20261120.json`.

## What the replacement stage buys

Bounded protocol: candidates = union(dense top-20, full-query CE top-20) minus the frozen
top-10, ordered by best rank, capped at 3; each candidate compared with ranks 8/9/10 in
both presentation orders; at most one swap per query; fixed threshold 0.

## What the reviewer's seven items produced

1. **Fair reranker control**: the padding bug is fixed (left padding, official
   `<think>` suffix kept outside truncation, the same 112-word query-matched passage).
   The control is "not better, interval includes zero"; the earlier "clearly worse"
   claim is retracted in every document.
2. **Query reasoning**: Qwen3-4B analyses over all 519 dev queries give 0.2933 (original)
   vs 0.2930 (instruction + analysis) vs 0.2546 (analysis alone) vs 0.2915 (short query).
   Neutral at this scale; the published TEMPO-style lift needs GPT-4o analyses with a
   different retriever and does not reproduce.
3. **FollowIR p-MRR**: negative on all three collections (-0.0260/-0.0368/-0.0433 on
   Core17, -0.0094/-0.0236/-0.0208 on News21, -0.0449/-0.0214/-0.0347 on Robust04),
   with no promoted document in any cell. The nDCG transfer (+0.0084/+0.0072/+0.0064) is
   ranking quality, not instruction following.
4. **Error analysis on held-out train** (969 queries ranked by cross-encoders that never
   saw them): 8,518 non-relevant top-10 slots, 5,161 with a relevant document in the
   dense top-100, 586 queries improvable by one swap, gain shares 44.7/34.1/21.2 across
   ranks 1-3/4-7/8-10 (dev: 43.6/35.0/21.4). Model-assisted labels on 100 stratified
   slots: temporal 50, entity scope 38, numeric 5, exclusion 4, comparison 1, none 2.
5. **Expanded action space** (top-100 candidates, any of the ten slots, no-op allowed) at
   2/6/12/18 prompts: -0.0009/-0.0016/-0.0054/-0.0057 on the fused start and
   +0.0026/+0.0012/+0.0011/+0.0006 on the dense start, i.e. widening loses most of what
   the bounded protocol gains on both systems.
6. **Expected-gain models**: ridge on 8,721 leak-free fold pairs reaches AUC 0.66 in-fold
   but scores 0.3615 (+0.0034, CI [-0.0015,+0.0088]) on dev; allocation replays show every
   policy between -0.0008 and -0.0057 while oracle ordering is +0.0003 and oracle
   decision +0.0593, so allocation is not the bottleneck.
7. **Acceptance**: not met. The condition question is precise on RETECO (+0.0044, 80% of
   its swaps helpful) but does not beat Direct at equal budget on the fused start
   (paired +0.0015, CI [-0.0001,+0.0044] at 2x prompts), is weaker than Direct on the
   dense and reranker starts, and on FollowIR moves the score by 0.0000/+0.0019/+0.0019
   while removing demoted documents in 0/2, 2/7 and 2/7 of its swaps.

## Negative results worth keeping

Context (+77% tokens, no gain); logistic gate (tau equals the smallest eligible
calibration probability, i.e. filters nothing; recalibrated tau 0.1026 gives +0.0009 on
dev); cheap-gap allocation (random matches it at k=3); widened action space; top-slot
window (-0.0042 vs +0.0065 for the tail); combined relevance+condition prompt; cascade
second opinion; evidence-first generation; two-judge agreement and veto; cost-neutral 6/3
budget split (+0.0001); learned expected-gain model; absolute condition filter (3 neutral
swaps).

## Where that leaves the paper

The defensible claim is: a bounded, single-swap local comparison stage improves a strong
fused retrieval system by +0.0065 (CI [+0.0021,+0.0117]) at 18 judge prompts per query,
the gain reproduces across three starting systems (+0.0065/+0.0106/+0.0131) and is
positive on three FollowIR collections, the bidirectional agreement rule is load-bearing,
and every attempt to make the judgement itself more reliable failed. That is a SemEval
system-paper contribution with a documented negative programme, not a new
constraint-reasoning method.
